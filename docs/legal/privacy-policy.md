# Privacy policy

Movie Mates helps two people find something to watch: each of you likes titles privately, and the app shows
you the titles you both like. This policy says what we collect, why, who else sees it and how to delete it.
The short version: **your Likes are private, we don't sell or share your data, and you can delete everything
from inside the app.**

Movie Mates is run by [operator name]. Contact: [contact address].

## What we collect

- **Your account:** your email address, your name, your photo if you add one, and how you sign in (an email
  link, Google or Apple).
- **What you do in the app:** your Likes, your own lists, your connections, your shared match lists and what
  you marked as watched together, your invites and notifications.
- **Your settings:** country, language for titles, genres, hide adult titles, notification choices and
  muted connections.
- **Technical data:** your IP address and app version in our server logs, and your phone's push token if you
  allow notifications.

We don't collect your location, your contacts, your watch history from other apps, or anything for
advertising.

## Why

Only to run the app: to sign you in, find matches between you and your connections, keep your devices in
sync, send the notifications you chose, and keep the service secure (for example, limiting how fast one
device can call it).

## Who can see what

- **Your Likes** are private. A connection only learns about a Like when they like the same title: that's a
  match. Nobody sees when you liked something.
- **Your own lists** are only ever seen by you.
- **A shared match list** is seen by its two people only.
- **Your connections** see your name and photo, and the matches you share. Nobody else can find you in the
  app: there's no search for people and no public profile.
- **Someone you send an invite to** sees your name and photo. If they decline, you're not told.

## Other services we use

- **TMDB** (The Movie Database) provides the movie and TV information. Your device or our server asks TMDB for
  what you search for and the titles you open, and for suggestions based on your list names. TMDB never
  gets your email or your account.
- **YouTube** plays trailers, in its privacy-enhanced mode.
- **Google and Apple**, if you sign in with them.
- **[Email provider]** sends the sign-in emails.
- **Apple's and Google's push services** deliver notifications to your phone, including their text (a
  connection's name and a title).
- **[Hosting provider]** runs our server and database for us.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising tools.

## How long we keep it

- Your account and its data: until you delete your account.
- Notifications: 30 days. Invites: until they're used or expire (7 days). Sign-in links: 15 minutes.
- Server logs: 14 days. Backups: 30 days.

## Your choices

- **Download your data** in Settings → Account, as one file.
- **Delete your account** in Settings → Account. It's immediate, and it removes your Likes, lists,
  connections and shared lists. Copies in backups are gone within 30 days.
- **Change your name and photo** on your profile, mute a connection, or end it at any time.
- Wherever you live, you can ask us what we hold about you or to correct it: [contact address].

## Children

Movie Mates is for people 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll
delete it.

## Security

Connections to our server are encrypted. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes, and there are
no passwords to leak.

## Changes

We'll say in the app before a change to this policy takes effect, and the date above changes with it.
